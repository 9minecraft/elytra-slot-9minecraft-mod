# Elytra Slot — Never Swap Your Armor for Wings Again

Elytra Slot 9minecraft Mod gives you a dedicated inventory space just for your wings. Normally, you have to pick between wearing a strong chestplate for defense or wearing an elytra to fly. This mod changes that rule by adding a brand new slot next to your armor. You can finally wear your toughest chestplate and keep your elytra ready to go at the exact same time. It saves you from constantly opening your inventory to swap items when you want to take off or fight monsters.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Luca Argolo**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
